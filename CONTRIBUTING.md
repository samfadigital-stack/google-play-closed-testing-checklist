# Contributing to Google Play Closed Testing Checklist

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **Google Play Closed Testing Checklist**! Our goal is to maintain the most accurate, up-to-date, and battle-tested operational checklist for Android developers worldwide.

## How You Can Contribute

1. **Policy Updates**: Notice a recent change in Google Play Console closed testing policies or API requirements? Open a PR with links to official Google documentation.
2. **Operational Edge Cases**: Encountered device fragmentation issues, specific OEM permission traps, or opt-in synchronization delays? Add tips to the relevant checklist phase.
3. **Questionnaire Clarity**: Suggest improved phrasing or critical evidence categories for the production access questionnaire.

## Pull Request Guidelines

- Ensure your contributions are objective, developer-focused, and verified against real-world Google Play Console behavior.
- Cite official Google Play Developer documentation where applicable.
- Keep markdown formatting clean and consistent with the existing repository structure.

## Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors regardless of background or experience level. Be respectful and constructive in all discussions.
